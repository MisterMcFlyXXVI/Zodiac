# I1 deviations from the pre-registered plan (logged before any measurement ran)

Plan: `hypotheses/candidates/z13_circles_image_protocol_PLAN.md`. `segment.py` is in `scripts/z13_circles/`.

1. **Cipher-line boxes** were found from ink gaps along the line (`segment.py`: column projection,
   exactly 13 spans), not drawn by hand. Stricter than the plan (no judgement), same intent.
2. **Same-symbol handwriting pairs.** The plan asked for "repeated handwritten capitals ... the first 10
   letters that occur at least twice in the letter's first line of body text". That line ("This is the Zodiac
   speaking") has only two capitals (T, Z), neither repeated. Used instead the letters that repeat in that
   line, case-sensitive: h, i, s, e, a (the only five), first two occurrences each -> 5 pairs.
3. **Joined letters** (h joined to T in "This"; e and a joined in "speaking") were boxed by hand at split
   columns chosen on a 6x zoom; the T's crossbar is excluded from the h box (top of the h ascender cut).
4. Reference sets therefore: S = 3 cipher pairs (A-A, N-N, M-M) + 5 handwriting pairs = 8 pairs;
   X = 33 different-symbol cipher pairs + 3 circle-vs-crosshair pairs = 36 pairs. S is small.
