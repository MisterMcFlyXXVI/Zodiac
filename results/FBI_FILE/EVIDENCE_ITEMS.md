The items that matter for Z13 and Z32 are Qc45-Qc47 (20 Apr 1970 envelope and two-page letter with the
13-symbol cipher) and Qc51 (26 Jun 1970 envelope, map and 32-symbol cipher). The file holds only photocopies
of them; see CIPHER_PAGES.md. The lab numbers come from the FBI's own worksheets, so they are the numbers to
quote in a records request for the original photographs.
