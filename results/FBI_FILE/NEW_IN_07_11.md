# What parts 07-11 add to the Commons parts 1-5
Method: word-trigram overlap of OCR text against Commons parts 1-5 (index.csv has the per-page data).
- Part 07 (40 pp): 2017 email correspondence about a FOIA request and an interview request for a documentary; 40 of 40 pages new.
- Part 08 (33 pp): 2020-21 FBI Lab communication logs, a 2021 lab report and emails on the 340 cipher, with a typed solution text (p3, p10, p16-17, p23); all new.
- Part 09 (297 pp): all new except p273-275. p1-~35 outside analysis essays and newspaper clippings; p35-43 letters (1980s); p44-117 typed essays by an outside analyst (bus bomb, cards, Morse/number theories); p118-167 county sheriff reports on a 1969 incident; p168-262 PHOTOCOPIES OF THE EVIDENCE (Qc items: envelopes, letters, cards, including Z13 p209-211 and Z32 p215); p263-297 later letters and essays.
- Part 10 (170 pp): all new. 1970 newspaper pages (p1-3), police reports and a letter exchange (p30-60), a long outside-analyst essay (p63-135), later pages (p138-170).
- Part 11 (368 pp): 76 pages overlap Parts 1-5 (lab reports and inventories: p106-122, 140-154, 156-172, 178-189, 192-199, 202-216, 223-258, 268-367, partly). The rest is new: handwriting specimens (p14-100), airtel/teletype traffic, and lab reports on 1970-74 items including the Qc52-63 section.
