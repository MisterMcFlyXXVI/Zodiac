# Analyst transcriptions of Z13 / Z32 symbols in the FBI file

Result: no FBI analyst (typed or handwritten) transcription of the Z13 or Z32 symbols was found in Parts 1-5 or 7-11.
- FBI lab pages on the 20 Apr 1970 item (F3 p169-173, F5 p191-194, P11 p239) only say "No decryption could be effected for the ciphertext portion of Qc46"; read at full resolution (F3 p172, F5 p194): no symbols copied.
- Hand-copied/handwritten cipher pages by analysts (F5 p106-114, p171, p233) are for the 408/340-type ciphers and plain-text hand copies, not Z13/Z32.
- F5 p93 (police cover letter) lists the 13-character letter as an enclosure: "My name is ___"; no symbols.

No typed Z32 transcription found (only the photocopy P09 p215).

Summary: the file contains no FBI code-breaker transcription of Z13 or Z32. The four third-party typed renderings
of Z13 (P09 p76; P10 p71, p72, p129) write the three circled positions with one and the same symbol, which
is a reader's notation and not evidence about the original. Page numbers are PDF pages; see README.md.
